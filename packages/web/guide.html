Step-by-Step Setup

Getting Started with Focussive

Follow these quick steps to configure the Android app and Chrome Companion extension for seamless deep work.

1

Install Focussive on Android

Download the official Focussive app from the Google Play Store on your Android phone or tablet:

  • Install from Play Store: Open Focussive on Google Play and tap Install.
  • Usage Access Permission: When prompted on first launch, enable Usage Stats access so Focussive can recognize when distracting apps are foregrounded.
  • Accessibility Service: Grant the Accessibility permission to allow Focussive to show the calming focus shield overlay over blocked apps during active sessions.
  • Battery Exemption: Allow Focussive to run without aggressive OS battery optimization so your timers stay accurate when the screen is locked.
Tip: All app evaluations happen locally on your phone. Focussive never uploads your screen or application usage history to external servers.
2

Install Focussive Companion on Google Chrome

Extend your focus defense to your desktop workspace by installing the official browser extension:

  • Chrome Web Store: Navigate to the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.
  • Pin to Toolbar: Click the extensions puzzle icon in Chrome’s top bar and pin Focussive so you can easily check session status at a glance.
Seamless Sync: The companion extension runs lightweight in the background and only blocks websites during scheduled or active focus sessions.
3

Log In with the Same Account

Pair your phone and computer together using your Focussive account credentials:

  • Open Focussive on Android and sign in with your email or Google account.
  • Open the Focussive Companion popup in Chrome, select the Email or Google tab, and log into the same account.
  • Within milliseconds, your devices establish an encrypted, real-time WebSocket sync channel.
Check Connection: Once logged in, the Chrome extension popup displays a green "Synced" indicator confirming two-way communication.
4

Customize Your Distraction Blocklists

Choose which websites and apps tempt you most during deep work and set strict blocking rules:

  • Mobile Blocklist: In the Android app, select social media apps, games, and short-form video apps you want quarantined.
  • Desktop Blocklist: In the Chrome extension (or Android settings), configure distracting websites (e.g., reddit.com, facebook.com, instagram.com, x.com).
  • Synchronized Rules: Any rule set on your phone instantly reflects on your browser, so you cannot escape from mobile to desktop.
5

Apply the "Driving Forces" Protocol

Willpower alone is fragile. Before starting each session, Focussive prompts you for intentionality:

  • Select a Motivation Category: Categorize your work under Career, Craft, Clarity, or Health.
  • Write a Re-Anchoring Phrase: Enter your core reason for staying on task (e.g., "Finish client draft so I can relax tonight guilt-free").
  • Cognitive Interruption: If you accidentally type a blocked domain in Chrome, Focussive displays your own re-anchoring phrase instead of an angry block screen, gently steering you back to deep work.
ADHD & Neurodivergent Design: Purposeful friction turns impulsive reflex loops into conscious, thoughtful choices.
6

Start a Session & Maintain Flow

You're ready to enter uninterrupted deep work:

  • Set your timer duration (e.g., 25 min Pomodoro, 50 min Deep Block, or 90 min Flow state) and tap Start Focus on either your phone or Chrome.
  • Both devices lock down simultaneously. Restricting distractions on one immediately seals escape routes on the other.
  • Emergency Unblock: Need to look up a two-factor code or urgent email? Focussive includes a strictly limited 60-second emergency unblock with a mandatory cool-down to prevent abuse.
