Cognitive Foundations

Designed with Behavioral Science

Focussive was built around peer-reviewed principles of attention management and habit formation, addressing the true psychological root causes of distraction.

Attention Residue Mitigation

Research by Dr. Gloria Mark and Dr. Sophie Leroy proves that switching to another screen leaves lingering attention residue, taking up to 23 minutes to regain deep focus. Focussive synchronizes all devices simultaneously to eliminate cross-device leaks.

Leroy (2009) • Mark (2014)

Implementation Intentions

Psychologist Peter Gollwitzer demonstrated that pre-committing to specific motivations and conditional plans significantly increases goal adherence. Focussive's "Driving Forces" activate deliberate agency when impulse strikes.

Gollwitzer et al.

Calibrated Micro-Friction

Impulsive screen pickups are automatic System 1 reflexes. Introducing purposeful, non-punitive friction pauses the subconscious impulse and re-engages deliberate prefrontal executive control (System 2).
