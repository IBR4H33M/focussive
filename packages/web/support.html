Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I pair the Chrome Extension with my Android Phone?

Open the Focussive app on your phone, go to Settings → Browser Extension, and select either Scan QR Code or One-Time Code. On your computer, click the Focussive extension icon and scan the code or enter the 6-digit PIN. Your devices will pair instantly.

2. How does website blocking work on Chrome?

When you start an active focus session on your Android phone, your session rules automatically synchronize to the Chrome extension. Any websites included in your blocked group will display the calm Focussive pause overlay until the session concludes.

3. Why is an emergency unblock available?

Focussive uses calibrated micro-friction. If you urgently need critical information from a blocked tab, you can take a temporary 1-minute unblock. This prevents panic while breaking the automatic scrolling impulse.

4. How do I redeem the 3-month free Pro beta code?

In the Focussive mobile app, navigate to Settings → Redeem Voucher, enter the code SHIPATON26, and tap Apply. Your account will immediately be granted 3 months of complimentary Pro access.

5. How can I request account or data deletion?

You can delete your account directly inside the mobile app settings under Account → Delete Account, or send an email to contact@solase.studio with your account email address. All user data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
